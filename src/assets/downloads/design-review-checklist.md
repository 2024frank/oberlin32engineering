# Design Review Checklist

- Can the team state the problem without describing its preferred solution?
- Has the team spoken with or studied the intended user, partner, or operating context?
- Is the semester finish line specific and testable?
- Which assumptions carry the most risk?
- What evidence supports the chosen architecture?
- What alternatives were considered and rejected?
- How do mechanical, electrical, software, cost, safety, and usability decisions interact?
- What can fail, and what happens when it does?
- What test will be run first?
- What result would cause the team to change direction?
- Is the budget justified and staged around learning?
- Are files, decisions, and responsibilities documented clearly enough for another student to continue the work?
