# 3-2 Engineering Advising Questions

Use this sheet to prepare for official advising. Requirements and partner-school policies can change, so confirm every individual plan through Oberlin and the relevant partner institution.

## Academic plan
- Which Oberlin courses are required for my intended engineering direction and partner school?
- Which courses should I complete first because they unlock later requirements?
- How do AP, IB, transfer, study-away, or summer credits apply?
- What course load is realistic across my remaining Oberlin semesters?
- Which Oberlin major, minor, or liberal-arts interests can fit my plan?

## Partner institutions
- What are the current eligibility and application requirements for each partner?
- Which institutions support the engineering field I am considering?
- How do calendars, housing, financial aid, and transfer timing differ?
- What should I know about guaranteed admission language versus competitive review?
- Who can connect me with recent students or alumni from each pathway?

## Timeline
- When should I declare, meet with the program director, and begin partner-school research?
- What deadlines apply during my second and third years?
- What documents, recommendations, or approvals should I prepare early?
- How should I plan for the transition away from Oberlin?

## Career and experience
- Which internships, research experiences, projects, or campus roles would strengthen my preparation?
- What technical skills should I build before transferring?
- How can I explain the liberal-arts and engineering combination to employers?
