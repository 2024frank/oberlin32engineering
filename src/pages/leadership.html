Working expectations

Titles are secondary to follow-through.

Founding officers should be willing to make small commitments, communicate early when something changes, and ask for help before a task becomes a crisis.

01

Show up prepared

Read the agenda, complete agreed tasks, and bring decisions or questions to the meeting.

02

Share the work

Document contacts, files, budgets, project status, and lessons so knowledge does not stay with one person.

03

Use honest language

Do not describe an idea as confirmed, a proposal as active, or a conversation as a partnership.

04

Design for access

Consider time, cost, disability access, prior experience, and the needs of students outside the usual technical circles.

A note for first-year students

You are allowed to apply.

Some roles may benefit from experience, but new students can bring energy, continuity, and a fresh view of what is confusing. The selection conversation will focus on reliability, capacity, and willingness to learn.
