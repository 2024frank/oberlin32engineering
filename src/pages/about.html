About the society

A meeting place for Oberlin’s engineering-minded students.

The society connects students across departments through hands-on projects, 3-2 planning, workshops, and conversation.

Why this club

Engineering interest here crosses departments.

A student interested in engineering might be studying physics, computer science, mathematics, chemistry, environmental studies, TIMARA, economics, art, or something else entirely. That mix creates unusual project teams, but only if those students can find one another.

The society gives them a shared place to meet, compare what they are learning, and turn ideas into work they can test together.

The first year

Four priorities.

The club will concentrate on work that creates a useful foundation for the students who come after us.

Connect students

Build relationships across class years, departments, experience levels, and identities.

Make 3-2 clearer

Collect peer questions and point students to current official advising information.

Finish small projects

Choose scopes that fit a semester and document what worked, what failed, and what comes next.

Establish regular events

Run meetups, build sessions, workshops, and conversations with engineers and alumni.

Who it is for
  • A peer group for students interested in engineering.
  • A place to explore technical projects without needing prior experience.
  • A way to share questions, resources, and lessons across class years.
  • A club for students who want engineering to be part of their Oberlin experience.
One important boundary
  • The society can help students form better questions and share experience, but it cannot confirm academic requirements or admission.
  • Official decisions belong with Oberlin advisers and the partner engineering schools.
The founding idea

Make engineering visible on campus.

Kwaku started the society because the interest was already here, but the students were often working separately. The club is meant to make collaborators, project teams, and 3-2 conversations easier to find.
