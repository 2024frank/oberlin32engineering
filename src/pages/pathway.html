THE FIVE-YEAR MODEL

Two institutions.
One intentional education.

Oberlin describes the program as three years of liberal-arts study, including mathematics and sciences, followed by two years of accredited engineering coursework at an affiliated engineering school. Individual course plans and partner requirements must be confirmed through official advising.

Y1EXPLORE + START

Build the first foundation

Begin mathematics and science, explore engineering directions, meet the program director, and connect with peers early.

Y2DEEPEN + COMPARE

Strengthen the plan

Continue prerequisite sequences, begin comparing partner institutions, and develop technical experience through projects or research.

Y3COMPLETE + TRANSITION

Prepare the handoff

Finish the Oberlin plan, complete applications and decisions, document what future students should know, and prepare for the transition.

TRANSFER GATEConfirm requirements · apply · decide · transition
Y4ENGINEERING CORE

Enter the technical sequence

Begin the affiliated institution's engineering curriculum, build new community, and connect liberal-arts strengths to technical work.

Y5SPECIALIZE + LAUNCH

Finish and move forward

Complete advanced engineering study, major projects, career preparation, and the final academic requirements for the combined path.
