What to send

Tell us what you need or can offer.

  • A question about joining or a planned event
  • A project problem, tool, space, or mentoring offer
  • An alumni, faculty, engineer, or researcher willing to speak
  • A campus organization interested in co-hosting something concrete
  • A correction to a resource or 3-2 link
  • An accessibility concern

Academic questions: We can help you prepare questions and find official pages, but only authorized advisers and institutions can confirm requirements.

Send a message

Working with the society

Propose one concrete next step.

Include the audience, activity, timing, costs, access needs, and what you are asking students to do.

Speakers and mentors

Share a specific topic, your background, preferred format, available dates, and whether the conversation can be recorded or summarized.

Campus collaborators

Suggest one event or project with a clear owner, timeline, room or equipment need, and division of responsibility.

Material support

Describe the item, condition, safety information, restrictions, and whether the society would own, borrow, or dispose of it.
