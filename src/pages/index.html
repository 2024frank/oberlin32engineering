The Oberlin 3-2 Engineering Society is a new student group for anyone interested in engineering, whether you are committed to the 3-2 pathway or still figuring out where you fit.

The academic pathway

Three years at Oberlin. Two at an engineering school.

The society offers peer support and practical questions to ask. Official requirements always come from Oberlin and the partner institution.

1Build foundationsMath, science, writing, exploration
2Check requirementsCourse sequence and partner options
3Prepare the moveApplications, finances, housing
4Engineering studyPartner institution
5Complete both degreesPartner institution and Oberlin
