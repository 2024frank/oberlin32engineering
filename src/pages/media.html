About the society

The Oberlin 3-2 Engineering Society connects students across departments through engineering projects, 3-2 planning, workshops, and conversations with engineers and alumni. Membership is open to Oberlin students of any class year, field of study, or experience level.

Photography policy

Replace temporary imagery with documented society photographs.

The site currently uses generated workbench images and licensed engineering photographs. Once society activities begin, those images should be replaced with consented photographs from Oberlin meetings, projects, and events.

  • Get permission before publishing identifiable students.
  • Record the creator, source, and license for every image.
  • Avoid implying that placeholder subjects are society members.
  • Write alt text that describes what the image shows.
