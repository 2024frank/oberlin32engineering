QUESTIONS TO ASK EARLY

Planning is not only
a course checklist.

A complete decision includes academic, financial, personal, and professional questions. Use these prompts to prepare for advising conversations.

01

Academic fit

Which engineering fields interest me? What courses and grades are expected? How do requirements differ by partner institution?

02

Application path

What is the timeline? Which materials are required? What is guaranteed, conditional, or competitive?

03

Financial reality

How will tuition, aid, housing, travel, and other costs change during the engineering-school years?

04

Student experience

What support, community, research, internships, and advising will be available after the transition?

05

Career direction

What technical experience should I build at Oberlin now, and how can projects, research, and internships test my interests?
