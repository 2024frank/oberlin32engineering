Purpose

Meet other Oberlin students interested in engineering.

Students with engineering interests study in different majors and class years. The society is being organized so they can find collaborators and compare 3-2 questions.

The founding group will decide which projects and events are realistic after it reviews student interest and available resources.

First-year tasks

  1. 01

    Recruit members from different departments and class years.

  2. 02

    Review proposals and select projects that fit the available time, tools, workspace, budget, and safety requirements.

  3. 03

    Collect 3-2 questions and maintain links to Oberlin and partner-school pages.

  4. 04

    Announce the first event after its organizer, date, room, and access details are confirmed.
