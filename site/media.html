Short description

About the society

The Oberlin 3-2 Engineering Society is a student group being organized for Oberlin students interested in engineering and the 3-2 program. Its planned work includes student projects, technical events, and links to advising resources. Membership interest is open to students in any major or class year.

Usage

Keep the mark intact.

  • Preserve the square proportions.
  • Leave clear space around the outer ring.
  • Use a calm, high-contrast background.
  • Do not recolor, crop, stretch, or redraw it.
Brand colors

Maroon, gold, and paper.

Maintain readable contrast and do not use color as the only way to communicate status.

Image sources

Current images are placeholders.

The website uses generated images and licensed stock photographs. Photos from society activities will be added only with permission.

  • Ask identifiable students before publishing their image.
  • Record the creator, source, and license.
  • Do not describe stock or generated subjects as society members.
  • Describe meaningful images in alt text.
