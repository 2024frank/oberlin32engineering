Every event has an engineering brief

Purpose. Owner. Action. Follow-through.

The event itself is only one part of the work. Strong preparation and clear follow-up turn attendance into value.

01

Problem

What student need is this event designed to address?

02

Audience

Who should be in the room, and how will they know it is for them?

03

Action

What can participants do during or immediately after the event?

04

Artifact

What notes, recording, guide, contact, or resource remains useful later?

05

Feedback

What evidence will tell us whether the event should change or return?
