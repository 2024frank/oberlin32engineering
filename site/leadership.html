HOW WE LEAD

Authority with
visible responsibility.

Every leadership role owns clear outcomes and documentation. Decisions should be understandable, work should be shared, and information should not disappear into private accounts or one person's memory.

01

Own a system

Each officer is responsible for a recurring area, not only a title. Membership, finance, projects, programs, communications, and partnerships need clear owners.

02

Publish the work

Calendars, project decisions, event plans, budgets, templates, and post-event learning should be organized and accessible to the next board.

03

Develop successors

Strong leaders invite younger members into meaningful work early and prepare them to take responsibility before a transition becomes urgent.

04

Measure the mission

Activity is not impact. Each board should track participation, project progress, resource use, partnerships, and what members actually gained.

OPEN LEADERSHIP ROLES

The founding team
is still forming.

Leadership experience is useful, but commitment, follow-through, communication, and willingness to learn matter more. Students from every class year and discipline are encouraged to express interest.

What every officer commits to

Regular executive-board meetings, clear ownership, reliable communication, shared documentation, responsible use of funds, and a serious transition process at the end of the term.
