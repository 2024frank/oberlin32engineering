Overview

The Oberlin 3-2 Engineering Society collects only the information needed to respond to messages, manage participation, review submissions, run programs, and improve the organization. We do not sell personal information.

Information submitted through forms

Forms may collect names, email addresses, interests, project ideas, leadership preferences, event suggestions, opportunity submissions, competition interests, and other information you choose to provide. Public forms on this website are stored in the society's configured content system. The membership Google Form is governed by Google's terms and the settings applied by the form owner.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to you, organize member participation, evaluate submissions, plan events and projects, send requested updates, and maintain responsible organizational records.

Public content

Leadership profiles, projects, news, competition entries, and other public content are published only through the website's authorized administration process. Student directory information should not be published without consent.

Data security

The production content system is designed to use authenticated administrator accounts and row-level database policies. No system is perfectly secure, so sensitive information should not be submitted through general website forms.

Requests

To ask about information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion where appropriate, email fkusiapp@oberlin.edu.

Academic information

The society's resource hub does not replace official Oberlin advising. Program requirements and policies should be verified through official College resources.
