THE BUILD SYSTEM

Projects need more
than a good idea.

Each team follows the same backbone while choosing methods that fit the problem. The point is not bureaucracy. It is stronger decisions, fewer invisible assumptions, and a record future teams can use.

01

Frame

Define the user, context, need, constraints, and what evidence would change the direction.

02

Scope

Choose a finish line that is ambitious enough to matter and small enough to reach this term.

03

Review

Present the approach before major spending or construction. Invite technical and user criticism early.

04

Build + test

Prototype, measure, break, learn, revise, and make the evidence visible.

05

Document + show

Publish the work, explain limitations, preserve files, and demonstrate the system in public.

WHAT STRONG PROJECTS SHARE

Good engineering is
legible.

USER

A specific need

The project can name who benefits, what changes, and how the team learned about the problem.

TEST

A measurable claim

Teams define success before the final demonstration and report results without hiding weak evidence.

SYSTEM

Integrated decisions

Mechanical, electrical, software, cost, safety, ethics, and usability choices are treated as one system.

RECORD

A useful archive

Another student should be able to understand the design, reproduce key work, and continue from the documented next step.
