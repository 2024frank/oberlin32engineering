When a project is selected

Its brief will name the scope, lead, team, resources, and first milestone.

Selection criteria

Members will choose the first projects.

Proposals will be reviewed for their value to a 3-2 application, usefulness on campus, cost, workspace, tools, supervision, and safety.

Useful for a 3-2 application

Will the work produce a design, prototype, code, measurements, test results, or another result a student can explain?

Useful on campus

Who at Oberlin would use the result, and what problem would it address?
