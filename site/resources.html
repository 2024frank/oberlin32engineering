Library standard

What belongs here.

Resources must have a named source, a working link, and a stated reason for including them.

  • Prefer official Oberlin, partner-school, government, or recognized educational sources.
  • Show a last-reviewed date for time-sensitive guidance.
  • Separate student tips from institutional requirements.
  • Remove dead, misleading, duplicated, or outdated links.
  • Link to the original guide rather than reproducing it.
