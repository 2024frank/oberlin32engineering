Publication standard

Useful beats impressive.

A resource stays in the library only when it has a clear source, a working link, a short explanation, and a reasonable reason to trust it.

  • Prefer official Oberlin, partner-school, government, or recognized educational sources.
  • Show a last-reviewed date for time-sensitive guidance.
  • Separate student tips from institutional requirements.
  • Remove dead, misleading, duplicated, or outdated links.
  • Do not copy full copyrighted guides when a direct link is available.
