Oberlin 3-2 guide

Planning the 3-2 pathway.

Oberlin describes a route that begins with three years of liberal-arts study and continues at an affiliated engineering school. Each partner sets its own prerequisites, application rules, costs, and degree structure.

Planning timeline

Begin with the course sequence.

Map Oberlin’s math and science sequence against the prerequisites for the engineering field and partner school you are considering.

Before or during year 1

Start the math and science sequence

Discuss placement, calculus, physics, chemistry, computer science, and possible engineering fields with an adviser.

Ask: Which courses must be taken in sequence, and what happens if one is delayed?

Year 1 to year 2

Compare partner requirements

Build a table for each partner covering prerequisites, grade expectations, application dates, financial-aid processes, and degree options.

Ask: Which requirements apply to my intended major and entry year?

Year 2

Check the whole Oberlin degree

Review major, distribution, writing, residence, and other graduation requirements instead of looking only at engineering prerequisites.

Ask: What must be completed before leaving Oberlin, and what may be completed through transfer credit?

Year 2 to year 3

Plan the application and transition

Confirm application materials, recommendations, transcripts, housing, transportation, visa implications where relevant, and financial aid.

Ask: What deadlines are controlled by Oberlin, and which are controlled by the partner school?

Year 3

Finalize the degree and application plan

Before leaving Oberlin, confirm the course record, remaining degree requirements, admission status, aid package, and transfer-credit decisions.

Ask: Who should I contact when a requirement or transfer decision is unclear?

Advising meeting checklist

Bring specific questions.

  • My intended Oberlin major and possible engineering field
  • My current math and science placement
  • A draft course plan for the full route
  • Partner prerequisites I have checked
  • Questions about financial aid and total cost
  • Questions about housing, credit transfer, and degree timing
  • Backup options if I change fields or stay four years at Oberlin

After an advising meeting

Record who you spoke with, what was confirmed, the source link, and any unresolved questions.
