Future possibility

An Oberlin engineering showcase is being explored.

We are considering a future event where student teams could demonstrate projects and explain their design decisions. The format, date, venue, funding, and approval path are not confirmed.

Help evaluate the ideaSee current project proposals
Idea under evaluation

There is no registration, judging panel, prize, venue, or event date at this time.

What would have to be true

We should not announce a competition before the basics exist.

A public event becomes responsible only when the organization can support the teams, the space, the safety requirements, and the follow-through.

  • A confirmed student organizing team
  • A faculty or staff approval path
  • A suitable venue and realistic schedule
  • Clear safety and equipment rules
  • A budget and source of support
  • Enough active teams to make the event worthwhile
  • A fair review process and accessibility plan
Possible format

A showcase first, competition only if it adds value.

The founding team should decide whether ranking teams would improve learning or simply add pressure. A demonstration night may be the better first event.

OPTION A

Open demonstration night

Teams share prototypes, tests, failures, and next steps without formal scoring.

OPTION B

Design review

Mentors give structured feedback on problem definition, evidence, engineering choices, and communication.

OPTION C

Small challenge

A limited prompt, common constraints, and transparent criteria, only after resources and oversight are confirmed.

Help make the decision

Interest is useful. Honest constraints are more useful.

Tell us what kind of event would help students, what you could contribute, and what risks or barriers the organizers should consider.
